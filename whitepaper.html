NETRA AI Whitepaper

Artificial Intelligence • BNB Smart Chain • Community Powered

Introduction

NETRA AI is a community-driven AI meme token built on BNB Smart Chain. The project combines blockchain, artificial-intelligence branding and public community development. NETRA AI is early-stage and focuses on transparency, documentation and gradual long-term execution.

Vision

The long-term vision is to establish a recognized AI-focused community on BNB Chain and progressively research practical AI tools and AI + RWA applications. Roadmap items are goals rather than guarantees and may change with technical, financial, market and regulatory conditions.

Market and transparency

NETRA trades through a public PancakeSwap V2 pool. Price, liquidity and activity are determined by the market. Holder concentration, including large independent wallets, may create substantial sell-pressure risk. Users should verify current liquidity, holders and transactions directly on BscScan and DEX Screener.
