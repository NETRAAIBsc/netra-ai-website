🚀 Introduction

NETRA AI is a community-driven AI meme token built on the BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20). Our vision is to combine Artificial Intelligence with blockchain technology to create an innovative, transparent, and community-powered ecosystem. NETRA AI focuses on long-term community growth, decentralized participation, and future AI-powered utilities.

🎯 Project Vision

Our goal is to build one of the most recognized AI meme communities on BNB Chain. NETRA AI aims to provide a transparent ecosystem where community members can participate in governance, future AI utilities, and ecosystem expansion. We believe AI and blockchain together will unlock new opportunities for decentralized innovation.
